# Freelance starter kit

## Portfolio samples

Open `index.html` in a browser. The two links show an English small-business landing page and an English–Indonesian version with a working language switch. The business is fictional. These are **concept samples**, not past client projects. Replace them with actual client work as you gain experience.

The samples use HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. They demonstrate page structure, responsive layout, and localized copy. They do not demonstrate WordPress or Wix implementation, so describe them accurately when applying to platform-specific work.

## Suggested profile headline

WordPress, Wix & bilingual website support | English–Indonesian

## Suggested profile summary

I build clear, mobile-friendly pages for small businesses using WordPress, Wix, HTML, and JavaScript. I can also adapt website content between English and Bahasa Indonesia so it reads naturally for each audience. I use AI tools to speed up drafts and ideas, then review the copy, layout, links, and final details myself. I currently take on focused landing pages, website updates, and localization projects.

Adjust the summary to match your own experience before publishing. Only claim a platform or feature when you can show or deliver it.

## Two offers to test

1. **One-page business website:** discovery, page structure, responsive design, contact section, basic handover. Confirm whether the client provides branding, copy, images, hosting, and a working form or booking tool before quoting.
2. **English–Indonesian website localization:** translate and adapt up to an agreed number of pages, preserve layout and links, and review both language versions on mobile. Confirm the direction of translation and who will approve final wording.

## Proposal draft: small-business website

Hi [name],

I can help build a clear, mobile-friendly site for [business]. I have worked with WordPress, Wix, HTML, and JavaScript, and I would start by agreeing on the pages and the main action you want visitors to take. I would then build the page, check it on mobile, test the links and forms, and provide a simple handover.

Here is a **self-initiated concept sample** showing my approach to a small-business landing page: [portfolio link].

Could you share your current site, the pages you need, and whether the text and images are ready? I can then propose a realistic scope and timeline.

## Proposal draft: bilingual website

Hi [name],

I can help adapt your site for English and Indonesian visitors. I work with both languages and can update the website as well as the wording, so the translated version fits the page and sounds natural to its audience. I will check navigation, calls to action, and mobile layout in both versions.

Here is a **self-initiated bilingual concept sample** with a language switch: [portfolio link].

Which pages need localization, and do you want English to Indonesian, Indonesian to English, or both?

## Weekly routine for 8–12 hours

- 2 hours: review new listings and save only those with a clear scope.
- 2 hours: write a few tailored proposals using the relevant sample.
- 6–7 hours: deliver paid work or improve platform-specific samples.
- 1 hour: track proposals, responses, hours spent, and what clients ask for.

Before accepting a larger site, confirm the actual availability and responsibilities of any front-end engineer you may collaborate with.
